## What Is Deskrawl?

Deskrawl is a fantasy idle action RPG developed by First Day Games. The game runs in a compact window along the bottom of the desktop, allowing players to follow their hero's activities while using other applications.

Its gameplay combines automatic combat, equipment collection, character builds, enemy capture, town development, and increasingly difficult challenges. Players choose a class, collect gear, improve their character, and prepare for stronger enemies as they advance.

The desktop format is central to the experience. Instead of requiring constant attention, Deskrawl lets players return to manage equipment, review loot, adjust abilities, and select their next objective.

## Essential Game Features

### Desktop Idle Combat

Deskrawl occupies a narrow area along the bottom of the screen. Heroes fight enemies and collect loot while players handle other tasks, returning to the game to review results and make decisions.

### Four Playable Classes

- **Warrior:** A close-combat approach built around direct engagements.
- **Wizard:** A magic-focused class with specialized abilities.
- **Monk:** A distinct fighting style shaped by its skills and equipment.
- **Hunter:** A ranged combat class suited to attacking enemies from a distance.

Each class has different strengths, and equipment choices affect how a character performs during encounters.

### Equipment and Loot Collection

Defeated enemies can yield equipment and resources. Players compare item attributes, replace weaker gear, and seek useful combinations for their preferred class.

Finding suitable equipment is an ongoing part of character development, especially when preparing for more demanding encounters.

## Hero Builds and Character Development

### Equipment Choices

Gear helps define a hero's combat capabilities. Players can adapt their equipment to suit their chosen class, current challenges, and preferred approach to combat.

### Skills and Synergies

Character development involves selecting abilities and combining them with suitable equipment. A coherent build can make encounters more manageable and help a hero advance into harder content.

### Long-Term Progression

As the hero becomes stronger, new challenges create reasons to revisit equipment choices and refine the character setup.

## Enemy Capture and Minions

### Capturing Enemies

Deskrawl includes a system for capturing eligible enemies, including bosses. Captured creatures become minions that contribute to the player's adventure.

### Loot Collection

Minions can help transport collected items using the loot cart, supporting the game's idle-oriented design.

### Passive Bonuses and Abilities

Some minions grant passive benefits, while others use special abilities during combat. Choosing suitable companions adds another layer to character development.

## Town Development

### Resource Management

Resources collected during gameplay can be invested in the town. Developing the settlement unlocks additional services and creates another path for long-term advancement.

### NPC Services

Non-player characters and town facilities expand the available management options as players progress. These services connect resource collection with equipment management and character development.

### Settlement Goals

Town upgrades give players objectives beyond combat. Players can decide how to use collected resources while balancing equipment needs and future challenges.

## Mythic Realms and Advanced Challenges

Mythic Realms provides demanding encounters, timed trials, and escalating difficulty tiers. These activities test the quality of a hero's equipment, abilities, and minion choices.

Higher difficulty levels challenge players to refine their builds and prepare more carefully before attempting tougher content.

## Trading and Item Economy

Deskrawl supports trading eligible items through the Steam Market. This adds an item-economy element to the equipment collection system.

Trading eligibility and availability depend on the item's status and the platform's applicable rules.

## System Compatibility and Performance

Deskrawl is listed for Windows PC. Published minimum requirements include an Intel Core i3 processor, 4 GB of RAM, a graphics card such as the NVIDIA GeForce GT 730 or AMD Radeon R7 240, and 5 GB of storage.

Recommended requirements include an Intel Core i5 processor, 16 GB of RAM, a graphics card such as the NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 or AMD Radeon R9 270, and 10 GB of storage.

Actual performance depends on hardware, display settings, background applications, and the installed game build. Check the current system requirements before installing.
